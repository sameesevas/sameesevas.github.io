I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.uta.edu" target="_blank">The University of Texas at Arlington (UTA)</a>, with research interests in AI for autonomous systems, autonomous vehicle safety and security, and real-time systems. My current research focuses on developing time-aware and safety-aware AI for autonomous systems. Previously, I worked as a Software Engineer and Innovator at <a href="https://research.samsung.com/srbd" target="_blank">Samsung R&amp;D Institute Bangladesh</a>. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the <a href="https://mist.ac.bd/" target="_blank">Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST)</a>. I am also passionate about competitive programming and problem-solving, having participated in more than 300 programming contests.
